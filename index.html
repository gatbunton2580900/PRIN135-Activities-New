Classic Fluffy Pancakes

a round, thick, and golden-brown breakfast cake with a tender, airy interior that springs back when pressed. It features slightly crisp edges, a mild sweetness, and a soft, cake-like crumb full of tiny air pockets that melt in your mouth.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp white sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 ¼ cups milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted (plus extra for cooking)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 ½ tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix the Dry Ingredients
  2. Whisk the Wet Ingredients
  3. Combine Wet and Dry
  4. Heat the Pan
  5. Pour and Cook
  6. Flip and Finish
